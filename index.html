03 / Process

How a project works.

A clear, structured process so you always know where things stand.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You tell me about your business and what you're looking for.

  2. 02

    Requirements

    We clarify scope, pages, features and any existing assets you have.

  3. 03

    Proposal & quote

    I review everything and send a clear quote — no surprises.

  4. 04

    Design & development

    I build your site with regular check-ins so you can see progress.

  5. 05

    Review & revisions

    You review the site and we refine the details together.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Your site goes live, tested across devices and browsers.

  7. 07

    Maintenance (optional)

    Ongoing support and updates if you need them after launch.
